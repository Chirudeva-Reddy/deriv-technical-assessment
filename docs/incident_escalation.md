# Incident Escalation

## Severity levels
Support classifies incidents into three severity levels. SEV1 means trading or withdrawals are unavailable for all customers. SEV2 means a feature is degraded or unavailable for a subset of customers. SEV3 covers a single customer issue with a workaround.

## Response targets
SEV1 incidents must be acknowledged within 5 minutes and the on-call engineering lead paged immediately. SEV2 incidents must be acknowledged within 30 minutes. SEV3 tickets are answered within 1 business day.

## Who to contact
Support agents escalate SEV1 and SEV2 incidents in the #incident-response channel and by paging the on-call engineer through the paging tool. Suspected account takeovers go directly to the security team regardless of severity.

## Customer communication
For SEV1 incidents, the status page must be updated within 15 minutes of acknowledgement and then every 30 minutes until resolution. A written post-incident review is published within 5 business days of any SEV1.
