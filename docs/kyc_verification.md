# KYC Verification

## Verification levels
Tradeport has two verification levels. Level 1 requires a verified email address and phone number and allows deposits but no withdrawals. Level 2 requires a government-issued photo ID and a proof of address, and unlocks withdrawals.

## Accepted documents
Accepted photo IDs are passports, national ID cards and driving licences. Proof of address must be a utility bill, bank statement or tax letter dated within the last 3 months. Screenshots and mobile phone bills are not accepted.

## Review times
Most Level 2 submissions are reviewed automatically within 10 minutes. Submissions flagged for manual review take up to 2 business days. If a document is rejected, the customer can resubmit immediately; after 3 rejections the case is escalated to the compliance team.

## Age and residency
Customers must be at least 18 years old. Tradeport does not onboard residents of countries on its restricted list, which is published on the legal page and reviewed every quarter.
