# Withdrawals

## Methods and processing times
Tradeport supports withdrawals by bank transfer, card refund and cryptocurrency. Bank transfers are processed within 1 to 3 business days. Card refunds take up to 5 business days to appear on the statement. Crypto withdrawals are broadcast within 30 minutes once approved.

## Limits
Level 2 verified accounts can withdraw up to 10,000 USD per day and 50,000 USD per calendar month. The minimum withdrawal is 20 USD for bank transfers and 10 USD equivalent for crypto.

## Fees
Bank transfers cost a flat 5 USD. Card refunds are free. Crypto withdrawals pay the network fee only, shown before confirmation.

## Security holds
Withdrawals are blocked for 24 hours after a password change or after 2FA is reset. A new withdrawal address must be confirmed by email and cannot be used for 48 hours after it is added.

## Same-method rule
Funds deposited by card must be withdrawn to the same card up to the deposited amount. Any profit above that amount is paid by bank transfer.
